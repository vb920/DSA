6-Day SDE Interview Revision Guide
Day 1: Array/String Patterns & Linear Data Structures
Focus: Two Pointer, Sliding Window, Hashing, Prefix Sum, Linked List, Binary Search.
Goal: Revisit fundamental efficiency techniques to gain confidence.
Day 2: Data Structure Implementation & Greedy
Focus: Stack, Queue, Heap, Greedy Algorithms, Interval Scheduling.
Goal: Master heap-based problems and greedy choices.
Day 3: Recursion & Hierarchical Data Structures
Focus: Recursion, Backtracking, Trees (Binary/BST), Tries.
Goal: Solidify tree traversals and backtracking frameworks.
Day 4: Graph Algorithms (Intense)
Focus: BFS, DFS, Dijkstra, Union-Find, Topological Sort.
Goal: Cover all major graph traversal and pathfinding techniques.
Day 5: DP & Bitwise Manipulation
Focus: Dynamic Programming (1D/2D), Bit Manipulation tricks.
Goal: Identify optimal sub-problems and bitwise efficiency.
Day 6: System Design & Range Queries
Focus: Design Data Structures (Design LRU/HashMap), Range Queries (Segment Tree/Fenwick Tree).
Goal: Bridge the gap between algorithmic thinking and system design
